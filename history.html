// 18 YEARS OF EXCELLENCE

Milestones in Global Decarbonization

From our humble inception in 2008 to deploying the world's most resilient offshore wind turbines and bifacial solar arrays.

200850 MW Installed

Founding & First Wind Installation

Enervox was founded with a mission to develop rugged, direct-drive turbines for high-wind northern regions. Completed our first 50MW onshore farm.

20131,200 MW Milestone

Expansion into Utility-Scale Solar

Launched our Solar Photovoltaic division and patented our dual-axis astronomical tracking system, boosting solar yield by 28%.

20185,000 MW Milestone

Pioneering Hybrid Storage & Microgrids

Constructed the first utility-scale hybrid wind-solar-BESS installation in Chile, setting global standards for continuous renewable baseload.

202212,000 MW Milestone

Deep-Water Floating Offshore Breakthrough

Commissioned our first tension-leg floating wind turbine array in the North Sea, operating in waters deeper than 120 meters.

202618,000+ MW Milestone

Global Leadership & 18GW Capacity

Operating in 34 countries with over 18GW installed capacity and 12,000 wind turbines in active generation. Leading next-gen hydrogen electrification.

// LOOKING AHEAD

Our 2030 Vision: 50GW Target

Aiming to power 40 million homes with next-generation deep-water floating turbines, tandem perovskite cells, and megawatt-scale green hydrogen electrolyzers.
