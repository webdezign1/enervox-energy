// OUR HERITAGE & MISSION

Pioneering the Transition to Zero-Carbon Energy

Founded in 2008 with a single onshore wind project, Enervox has expanded into a global powerhouse designing, constructing, and maintaining gigawatt-scale wind, solar, and hybrid storage arrays.

Our multidisciplinary teams of aerodynamicists, electrical engineers, and environmental scientists work in unison to eliminate fossil fuel dependency. We don't just supply equipment; we deliver turnkey infrastructure backed by 30-year performance guarantees.

Sustainable Innovation

Deploying high-efficiency TOPCon solar cells and gearless direct-drive turbines.

Tier-1 Reliability

Operating with 99.8% annual availability across four distinct continental grids.

Turnkey EPC Delivery

Single-contract accountability from land feasibility to grid interconnection.

Global Community Impact

Investing in local green workforce training and habitat preservation.
